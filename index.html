FAQ
Common questions
Is my data private? +
Yes. Everything is stored locally using IndexedDB in your browser. No account, no server, no analytics. Your decisions never leave your device.
What is the AI Advisor? +
The AI Advisor is an optional feature. You bring your own OpenRouter API key. It prompts a model to analyze tradeoffs, blind spots, and hidden assumptions — not to choose for you.
How is scoring calculated? +
Scoring is deterministic. Each scenario's metrics are multiplied by your weights, normalized, and aggregated into a composite score with uncertainty bands. No black boxes.
Can I use this on mobile? +
Yes, Pathwise is fully responsive. The dashboard and forms adapt to smaller screens while keeping the full functionality.
